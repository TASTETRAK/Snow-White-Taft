Our story

A Taft drive-in since the drive-in days.

Sno-White drive-ins have been part of Central Valley towns since the 1950s, and Taft's has sat on Center Street for as long as most regulars can remember. Around 2015 the building was rebuilt from the ground up with a stacked-stone front, a red tile counter and red booths inside, and the Sno-White sign went back over the door. Not much else changed.

Everything is still cooked when you order it. Burgers come single, double or triple, with pastrami, bacon, avocado or a western-style BBQ and onion rings. Crinkle cut fries get buried in chili, cheese and pastrami. The freezer side does milkshakes and malts, root beer and orange freezes, slushies, sundaes, banana splits and dipped cones. Pull through the drive-thru, walk up to the window, or grab a booth.
